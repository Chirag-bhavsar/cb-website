## What's in each spot
- **App screens + splash**: your real exported screens.
- **Audience avatars** — real photography, one distinct setting per
  persona so each feels true to who they are:
  - Rita (working mom) → cozy living-room portrait
  - Sneha (food blogger) → street-food vlogging shot, camera in hand
  - Ankit (bachelor) → relaxed apartment evening shot
  - Students & beginners → campus portrait, textbook in hand
- **Keshvi's persona portrait** → real office photo, matches her bio
  (marketing specialist, Pune).
- **Closing device shot** → reuses the splash screen as a bookend.
