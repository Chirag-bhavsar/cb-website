UX Case Study — Mobile App

A recipe for
effortless cooking.

CulinaryCart is a mobile-first app designed to make cooking more accessible and enjoyable — helping users explore recipes, convert ingredients into shopping lists, shop via integrated platforms, and share their culinary experiences.

UI/UX Design & Prototyping

Platform

Tools

Step 01 — Ingredients

Every dish starts with a problem worth solving.

The Spark — The Problem

Cooking a new dish should be exciting — not frustrating. But users often juggle between recipe blogs, grocery apps, and handwritten notes just to cook one dish.

  • Switching between apps to copy ingredients
  • No direct mapping between recipes and grocery items
  • Confusion with quantities, brands, or availability
  • Time-consuming switch between recipe and shopping apps
  • No link between recipe ingredients and shoppable items
  • Ambiguity around ingredient quantities or preferred brands
  • Lack of community features like feedback or sharing

The Vision — The Solution

CulinaryCart was envisioned as a unified platform that brings together recipe discovery, ingredient planning, grocery shopping, and social sharing.

  • Provide easy access to diverse recipes
  • Allow ingredient-to-cart conversion in one tap
  • Enable sharing and rating of cooking experiences
  • Empower users to explore recipes by preference, cuisine, or occasion
  • Auto-generate ingredient lists, mapped to real grocery products
  • Enable quick cart exports to platforms like Amazon Fresh
  • Let users rate, save, and contribute their own recipes

Step 03 — The Main Course

Meet Keshvi, our primary persona.

Keshvi recently moved to Pune for a new job and lives independently. She has a keen interest in clean eating and enjoys cooking as a way to relax after work. Between long work hours and weekend meetups with friends, she looks for convenience without compromising on quality.

Name: Keshvi Prabhakar

Age: 27

Occupation: Marketing Specialist

Location: Pune, India

Goals

  • Cook quick, nutritious meals at home
  • Explore healthy recipes based on dietary tags (low-carb, high-protein)
  • Avoid the hassle of multiple apps for recipe and grocery needs

Behaviors

  • Shops online from Amazon Fresh or Blinkit once a week
  • Saves recipes from Instagram or Pinterest but rarely gets back to them
  • Frequently looks for alternatives to high-sodium or processed ingredients

Pain Points

  • Doesn't always find accurate or clean ingredient suggestions in recipe apps
  • Finds switching between recipe and shopping apps time-consuming
  • Prefers visual step-by-step instructions but most platforms offer text-heavy pages

Preferences

  • Clean, image-led interfaces with tags and filters
  • Ability to substitute ingredients based on availability or nutrition
  • Integration with grocery delivery apps for seamless checkout

Final Course — Reflection

Final thoughts.

CulinaryCart reimagines the everyday cooking experience by connecting discovery with execution — in one intuitive platform. From uncovering the perfect recipe to sourcing ingredients and sharing the joy, every step was designed with empathy and ease in mind.

While this concept is still in the design phase, it has laid the groundwork for a unified culinary journey that balances user delight, utility, and innovation. This project was not just about building an app — it was about understanding habits, solving everyday frictions, and designing a seamless food experience people can truly enjoy.
